Poetry

Disabled-By Wilfred Owen

Disabled is a poem written by Wilfred Owen, a soldier and poet who fought in World War I. The poem explores the physical and emotional impact of war on soldiers, particularly those who have been injured or disabled as a result of their service. It highlights the contrast between the experiences of soldiers before and after their injuries, and the societal attitudes towards them.

The Poem

He sat in a wheeled chair, waiting for dark,
And shivered in his ghastly suit of grey,
Legless, sewn short at the elbow. Through the park
Voices of boys rang saddening like a hymn
Voices of play and pleasure after day,
Till gathering sleep had mothered them from him.
